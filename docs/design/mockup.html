The two hairline border pairs (border on bg, border on surface) sit below 3:1 in both themes. They are decorative separators between surfaces that already differ in tone, and every state that carries meaning is also shown in text or shape. The recommendation is to keep the exact palette and exempt these two pairs in the contrast test, documented in ThemePalette.h. If you prefer borders to pass 3:1 as well, the border tokens need a nudge, which changes the palette.

2. Mini mode, dot and pill

Always on top, never takes keyboard focus, no taskbar button. The badge background is the surface token at 88 percent in dark and 90 percent in light, over whatever is on screen. Ready sits at 55 percent opacity and rises on hover. No transcript text is ever shown here.

Dark theme, Hunter and Brass

Left of each pair is the dot style, 44 by 44. Right is the pill style, 168 by 40. The strip behind them is a simulated desktop, not a token. The default style is the dot. Hover the attention badge to read its message.
